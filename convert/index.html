How can this be free?

Because we never touch your files. Cloud converters pay for storage and bandwidth, so they cap you at 100 MB and upsell a subscription. Hog Convert ships a little software to your browser instead — after that, your own device does the work and costs us nothing. Bigger files just take longer. The video tools do carry one hard limit: they hold your source and the result together inside 2 GB of in-browser memory, so keep sources under 1 GB — or 1.8 GB when the output is audio only. That ceiling comes from the browser, not from a paywall.

Is it private?

As private as it gets. There is no upload step to intercept and no server copy to leak. Watch your network monitor while a job runs: apart from loading the page itself, nothing moves.

What's the catch?

Speed depends on your hardware, and very large videos need some RAM. A five-minute phone clip compresses in about its playback length on an average laptop. That's the honest trade: your minutes for your privacy.

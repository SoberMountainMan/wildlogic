The 3000×3000 preset explained

Digital distributors (RouteNote, DistroKid and friends) reject cover art that isn't exactly square at 3000×3000. This preset center-crops to square, scales to exactly 3000×3000, and exports high-quality JPG — the two boxes on their checklist ticked in one click. Pair it with WAV→FLAC and your release is submission-ready.

Square-cropping cuts the sides of non-square images — check the preview before shipping.

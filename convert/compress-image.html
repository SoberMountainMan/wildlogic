The 3000×3000 preset explained

Digital distributors (RouteNote, DistroKid and friends) reject cover art that isn't exactly square at 3000×3000. This preset crops to a square — centred by default, or drag the frame in the preview to pick your own region — scales it to exactly 3000×3000, and exports high-quality JPG. Pair it with WAV→FLAC and your release is submission-ready.

Square-cropping cuts away everything outside the frame — drag it over the part of the image that matters before shipping.

How it works

The enlargement uses Lanczos-class high-quality resampling applied in clean doubling steps, followed by an edge-aware unsharp pass executed on your GPU through WebGL. Everything runs locally: no upload wait, no file-size cap — a 20 MP photo takes about a second on any recent laptop.

This is honest upscaling: real detail can't be invented from nothing. For photos, artwork and screenshots, quality resampling plus smart sharpening gets you most of what paid tools deliver — at zero cost, with nothing leaving your device.

Distribution checklist this tool covers

  • ✅ Stereo (channels forced to 2)
  • ✅ Sample rate locked to 44.1 kHz
  • ✅ 16-bit depth (FLAC) or 320kbps (MP3)
  • ✅ Lossless FLAC compression — smaller than WAV, zero quality lost

Cover art still needed? Stores want exactly 3000×3000 — the image resize tool has a one-click RouteNote preset.

Why FLAC for release day?

FLAC is compressed like a ZIP but plays everywhere stores and streamers need it: mathematically identical to your WAV master at roughly half the size. MP3 320 is the accepted fallback when a platform won't take FLAC — transparent to most ears, but it discards information forever. Ship FLAC where possible.

    How it works

    Everything you paint gets handed to OpenCV's inpainting algorithm, which regrows the covered patch outwards from its surroundings. Great on timestamps, small logos, wires and blemishes over fairly uniform backgrounds. Big marks over busy texture come back soft or smudged — that's physics, not a paywall.

    First run downloads an engine

    The healing brain is about 9 MB and loads once, then lives in your browser cache. On a slow connection the first heal may take a minute to start — every later one is instant.

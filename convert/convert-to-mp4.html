Why re-encode instead of remux?

Sometimes an .mov or .mkv already contains H.264 video and only the container needs swapping — that takes seconds. But plenty of files carry exotic codecs that won't play outside VLC. This tool always produces clean H.264/AAC so the result plays everywhere, first time. If speed matters more than compatibility for your file, it still beats uploading 4 GB to somebody's queue.

Exotic codecs (some WMV variants, old DivX) may fail inside the browser engine — you'll get a clear error rather than a broken file.

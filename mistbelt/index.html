Mistbelt

a Hogsback hillside

Clear the alien trees off the hillside and plant the indigenous forest back. You get 12 action points a day and have 12 left today; they come back when the day rolls over.

Cutting one leaves its seed in the ground, so it comes back. That is why a cleared patch does not stay cleared.

Three mature trees around a patch and the shade shuts its seed bank for good. That is the whole game, and it takes seasons.

The bin takes 3 cuttings and the baboon trades them for the day’s token; the nursery is where you spend it. Hold a tile to ask what it is.

Mistbelt

a Hogsback hillside

Clear the alien trees off the hillside and plant the indigenous forest back. You get 24 action points a day and have 24 left today. One comes back every hour. Cutting spends them; planting is free.

Cutting one leaves its seed in the ground, so it comes back. That is why a cleared patch does not stay cleared.

Three mature trees around a patch and the shade kills the seed in that ground for good. That is how the hillside comes back \u2014 a patch at a time, and the birds follow the shade.

The bin takes 3 cuttings and the baboon trades them for the day’s token; the nursery is where you spend it. Hold a tile to ask what it is.

Your game saves itself on this device. Field notes → Save code carries it to another device, or keeps a copy in case this one is lost.
